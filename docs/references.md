# Scientific references

The implementation uses names from the following published GQ/H and dynamic soil-property literature. These references provide scientific background; they do not establish exact numerical equivalence between the implementation and any publication, or validation of this implementation by the publication's authors. Check units, assumptions, tabulation and interpolation independently for a particular application.

- Groholski, D. R., Hashash, Y. M. A., Kim, B., Musgrove, M., Harmon, J., and Stewart, J. P. (2016). "Simplified Model for Small-Strain Nonlinearity and Strength in 1D Seismic Site Response Analysis." *Journal of Geotechnical and Geoenvironmental Engineering*, 142(9), 04016042. [DOI: 10.1061/(ASCE)GT.1943-5606.0001496](https://doi.org/10.1061/(ASCE)GT.1943-5606.0001496).
- Darendeli, M. B. (2001). *Development of a New Family of Normalized Modulus Reduction and Material Damping Curves.* PhD dissertation, University of Texas at Austin. [University repository](https://repositories.lib.utexas.edu/items/efea68dd-895b-4f28-ac6e-71872ca5291a).
- Vucetic, M., and Dobry, R. (1991). "Effect of Soil Plasticity on Cyclic Response." *Journal of Geotechnical Engineering*, 117(1), 89–107. [DOI: 10.1061/(ASCE)0733-9410(1991)117:1(89)](https://doi.org/10.1061/(ASCE)0733-9410(1991)117:1(89)).
- Seed, H. B., and Idriss, I. M. (1970). *Soil Moduli and Damping Factors for Dynamic Response Analyses.* Earthquake Engineering Research Center, University of California, Berkeley. [National Technical Reports Library record](https://ntrl.ntis.gov/NTRL/dashboard/searchResults/titleDetail/PB197869.xhtml).
- Menq, F.-Y. (2003). *Dynamic Properties of Sandy and Gravelly Soils.* PhD dissertation, University of Texas at Austin. [University repository](https://repositories.lib.utexas.edu/items/2a7a8fec-45f1-4c81-920d-b1af79679e2e).
- Ishibashi, I., and Zhang, X. (1993). "Unified Dynamic Shear Moduli and Damping Ratios of Sand and Clay." *Soils and Foundations*, 33(1), 182–191. [DOI: 10.3208/sandf1972.33.182](https://doi.org/10.3208/sandf1972.33.182).

The code also names an "Idriss (1990) Rock" preset. A specific primary publication and exact tabulation for that preset have not yet been identified in this repository, so its bibliographic association and numerical behavior should be checked independently before use.
